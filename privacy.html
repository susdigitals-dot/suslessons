This Privacy Policy explains how (“we”, “us”, “our”), operating the website suslessons.in, collects, uses and protects your information when you visit the website, buy a digital product (e-books, notes, guides, checklists) or contact us. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address and/or phone number and message when you use the contact form or email/WhatsApp us.
  • Purchase information: when you buy a product, the payment platform shares your name, email address, phone number, the product bought and the order/transaction ID with us so we can deliver the product and give support.
  • Usage information: pages visited, links clicked, the referring website and a random session ID. We do not store your IP address and this data is not used to identify you.

We never receive or store your card number, UPI PIN, net-banking password or other payment credentials. Payments are processed entirely by our payment partner (for example Razorpay, Instamojo, Cosmofeed or Gumroad), which follows its own privacy policy and PCI-DSS security standards.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver the digital products you buy and send the download link and invoice.
  • To reply to your questions, support requests and refund requests.
  • To understand which pages and stories are useful, and improve the website.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We send marketing emails only if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Cookies and local storage

The website uses your browser’s local/session storage to keep a random session ID and simple preferences. We do not use advertising cookies. When you click an affiliate link (for example Amazon), that store may set its own cookies to track the referral — see our Affiliate Disclosure.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted services to run the website: Supabase (database), GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts and jsDelivr (fonts and scripts), our payment platform, and affiliate stores such as Amazon. These services process data under their own privacy policies. Links to other websites are not covered by this policy.

We keep purchase records as long as required by Indian tax law, contact messages for up to 2 years, and anonymous usage data for up to 1 year. We use reasonable security practices (encrypted HTTPS connections and access-controlled databases), but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
