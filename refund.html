This policy applies to digital products (e-books, notes, guides, checklists) sold by on suslessons.in.

1. Cancellations

Digital products are delivered immediately after payment, so an order cannot be cancelled once the payment is successful.

2. When you get a full refund

We will give a full refund if, within days of purchase, you tell us that:

  • you were charged but did not receive the product, and we could not deliver it within 48 hours of your request;
  • the file is corrupted, incomplete or does not open, and we could not send a working copy;
  • you were charged twice (or more) for the same order — the extra payment is refunded;
  • the product is materially different from its description on the website.

3. When refunds are not possible

Since a digital file cannot be “returned” once downloaded, we do not offer refunds for change of mind, buying by mistake after downloading, or not liking the content, except as required by law. Please read the description and the free sample (where available) before buying.

4. How to ask for a refund

Write to or use the contact form with:

  • your name and the email/phone used at checkout,
  • the product name and the order or transaction ID,
  • the reason (with a screenshot, if the file has a problem).

We reply within 2 working days.

5. Refund timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet). Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit. Once a refund is issued, the licence to use that product ends.

6. Affiliate purchases

Products bought from other stores through our affiliate links (for example Amazon) are sold by those stores. Returns and refunds for them are handled by that store under its own policy.
